# Synthetic practice problem: cooling a small instrument

This is an invented practice problem, not an actual UPC contest problem.

A sealed instrument is modeled as a spatially uniform body of heat capacity
C = 1200 J/K. Initially it is at 80 degrees Celsius. Ambient temperature
is fixed at 20 degrees Celsius. Heat is removed through a constant thermal
conductance G, with model C dT/dt = -G(T - 20 degrees Celsius).

Consider two stated design scenarios: G = 8 W/K and G = 12 W/K.
For each design, determine the time to reach 30 degrees Celsius and the
heat removed by that time. Explain which design is faster and why.
Identify the dimensionless relationship controlling the threshold time.
Assess sensitivity if each conductance differs from its stated value by
plus or minus 10 percent. These ranges are design scenarios, not measured
uncertainties or probability distributions. Discuss at least one physical
limitation of the stated model. Give a complete short English paper.

All model parameters and the evolution law above are stipulated for this
practice problem. No measured data, experiments or external calibration
are supplied. No team number or selected A/B problem is assigned.
